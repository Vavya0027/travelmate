About Us

Who We Are

We are a group of enthusiastic second-year BCA students from Teerthanker Mahaveer University, Moradabad, Section C. This website is part of our group project assigned by our professor to explore real-world web development skills.

Our Vision

Our goal is to create a digital tourist guide that helps travelers explore the most iconic destinations across India. Using interactive maps and curated content, we aim to make travel planning easier and more enjoyable for users.

What We Learned

Through this project, we gained practical experience in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, especially in integrating interactive maps using the Leaflet library. We also developed teamwork, project management, and creative thinking skills, which are essential in the IT field.
